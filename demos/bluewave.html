03 / Owner Growth

The same system can create a better front door for new management inventory.

Instead of a generic contact form, owners could enter property type, resort/community, bedroom count, current booking performance and management goals. Bluewave gets a qualified owner lead before the first conversation.

That creates leverage on both sides of the business: better guest conversion from current inventory and a cleaner owner-acquisition pipeline for adding new units.

FULCRUMHAUS / PROPOSED SYSTEM

Keep Escapia behind the scenes. Upgrade the experience in front of it.

This concept does not require Bluewave to throw away the reservation infrastructure it already uses. The stronger opportunity is a modern presentation and qualification layer that can sit in front of the current booking engine, increase direct-booking confidence and help acquire more managed inventory.
