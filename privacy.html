Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

What we collect

When you submit a project inquiry, FULCRUMHAUS may collect the information you provide, including your name, company, work email, selected engagement, budget range, timeline, and project description. Basic technical request information may also be processed to operate and protect the form.

How we use it

We use inquiry information to review your project, respond to you, evaluate fit, prepare potential next steps, protect the submission service from abuse, and maintain the reliability of our website. We do not sell inquiry information.

Service providers

Our website may be hosted by Vercel, and project notification email may be delivered through Resend. These providers process information as needed to provide hosting and email-delivery services on our behalf.

Retention

We retain inquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to evaluate and communicate about a potential engagement, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete inquiry information where applicable. To make a privacy request, use the project form and begin the project description with “Privacy request” so it can be routed appropriately.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information submitted through the website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy as our services or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
